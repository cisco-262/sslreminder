Please include your app version, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Do not send private keys, passwords, or sensitive server credentials.

What SSL Reminder covers

SSL Reminder monitors public SSL/TLS endpoints, including services on custom HTTPS ports. It tracks expiry, days remaining, issuer and subject information, certificate health signals, failed checks, and reminder status. It also supports PDF certificate detail reports plus import/export of monitored targets.

Common troubleshooting checks

  • Confirm the hostname is publicly reachable from the Internet.
  • If the service does not use port 443, verify that the custom HTTPS port is correct and publicly reachable.
  • Review hostname mismatch, self-signed, or untrusted-chain findings in Certificate Health.
  • If a cloud check fails, confirm DNS, firewall, TLS service availability, and the configured port before retrying.
