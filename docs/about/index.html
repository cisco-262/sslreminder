About SSL Reminder

SSL Reminder is a focused iPhone app for SSL certificate expiry reminders, built by OpsHome.

What SSL Reminder is

SSL Reminder helps developers, webmasters, and homelab users monitor public SSL/TLS certificate expiry dates. It keeps the product narrow on purpose: add certificate targets, review status, and receive push notifications before expiry.

Why it exists

Certificate expiry problems are small until they break a production site, API, dashboard, or personal service. SSL Reminder gives users a lightweight way to keep those dates visible without setting up a full monitoring system.

Part of the OpsHome product family

OpsHome builds practical tools for operations visibility. SSL Reminder is the focused certificate-monitoring utility in that family, while OpsHome NOC covers broader uptime, private-service, Docker Probe, Synology, Proxmox, Linux host, container, and homelab operations workflows.

Official product identity

The official SSL Reminder website is ssl.opshome.run. The iPhone app is distributed through the Apple App Store. These links identify the same SSL Reminder product published as part of the OpsHome product family.

Focused by design

SSL Reminder is intentionally narrower than a full monitoring platform. It focuses on public SSL/TLS certificate expiry, health signals, reminders, custom HTTPS ports, reporting, and target organization. For private infrastructure and full service monitoring, OpsHome NOC is the broader product.
