FAQ

Common questions

How many certificates can I monitor for free?

Up to 15 public SSL/TLS certificate targets.

Are the +50, +100, and +500 upgrades subscriptions?

No. They are one-time, non-consumable in-app purchases that permanently increase certificate capacity and can be restored through the App Store.

How do push notifications work?

SSL Certificate Monitor uses Apple push notification service. When a cloud check detects an expiry condition or supported check failure, the service can send an alert to your iPhone.

Can I back up and restore my monitored targets?

Yes. Import and export tools let you back up monitored certificate targets and restore them when moving to another device.

Can I export reports?

Yes. Certificate detail information can be exported as a PDF for review, documentation, or sharing.

Can I monitor non-standard HTTPS ports?

Yes, as long as the HTTPS endpoint and selected port are publicly reachable from the Internet.

Can it monitor private LAN or VPN-only certificates?

Cloud monitoring is designed for public SSL/TLS endpoints. Private-only targets may not be reachable from the cloud checker.
